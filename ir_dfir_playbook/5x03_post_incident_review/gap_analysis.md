## Priority summary

- **GAP-001 (P1):** The phishing typosquat captured credentials; time-of-click URL protection and credential-submission safeguards should prevent or disrupt this initial access path.
- **GAP-002 (P1):** Conditional Access suppressed identity risk signals because the device was compliant, allowing device posture to overshadow evidence of possible account compromise.
- **GAP-003 (P1):** PowerShell spawned MSBuild to execute the suspicious loader; application control and execution restrictions should block unapproved LOLBin use.
- **GAP-005 (P1):** Both compromised workstations exhibited C2-related connections; egress restrictions and blocking of confirmed malicious infrastructure should interrupt attacker communications.
- **GAP-007 (P1):** NTLM hash material was present in LSASS; Credential Guard and LSA protection reduce the risk of credential material being exposed to malicious processes.
- **GAP-009 (P1):** The attacker moved from WST-WS-031 to WST-WS-017 and accessed a laboratory system; workstation segmentation and restricted SMB paths should constrain lateral movement.
- **GAP-010 (P1):** The compromised `dmarsh` account read 14 patient laboratory report PDFs; least-privilege share permissions should limit PHI access when an endpoint or identity is compromised.
