# Executive Brief: MedDefense Patient Information Security Incident

**Prepared for:** Dr. Morales and the Board  
**Incident ID:** IR-2026-0420-01  
**Date:** 2026-04-20  
**Classification:** Internal — Confidential

## What happened

MedDefense is investigating unauthorized access to patient information following a security incident involving clinical workstations and a file server.

The investigation confirmed that 1,847 file-access events involving patient laboratory results and clinical notes occurred on the file server between April 19, 2026, at 02:14 UTC and 02:41 UTC. The records were accessed through an account assigned to a scheduling coordinator.

The investigation also identified suspicious activity on four clinical workstations and the file server. Ransomware preparation files were found on the four workstations, but the supplied evidence does not establish that those files were executed.

## What this means for patients

The confirmed records accessed include laboratory results and clinical notes. The number of unique patients affected has not yet been established. The available evidence does not confirm exactly which patient records, if any, were sent outside MedDefense.

## Regulatory posture

MedDefense is assessing its HIPAA breach notification obligations with Legal Counsel and the Privacy Officer; no conclusion that notification is unnecessary has been established.

## Actions taken and remaining work

The incident has been formally declared and forensic evidence has been collected from the initial affected workstation. The investigation is examining the affected systems, the patient information accessed, and the possibility of further unauthorized disclosure.

Remaining work includes confirming containment, determining the number of patients affected, assessing whether information left MedDefense, verifying the integrity of affected systems, and documenting the final notification decision.

## Next update

Dr. Morales will receive the next written status update within 60 minutes, followed by updates every 60 minutes while the incident remains active. Any material change in patient safety risk or the confirmed scope of affected information will be escalated immediately.
